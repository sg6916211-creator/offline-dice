HOW DOES OFFLINE DICE WORK?

Offline Dice powers custom digital dice rolls driven by an unbiased randomization engine that gives every color an identical chance of landing. Simply tap the 'ROLL AGAIN !' button whenever you're ready to spin the dice.

Customise your game setup by rolling anywhere from 1 to 12 dice at once. Each 6-sided die features six vibrant colors:
Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Purple.

ABOUT US

Offline-Dice.com was created to provide the ultimate digital dice simulator packed with custom color dice, interactive wheel features, and 100% fair randomization. Enjoy a perfectly smooth rolling experience designed for live streamers, gaming sessions, or any game of chance.

HOW DOES THE RANDOMIZATION WORK?

To guarantee 100% fair and completely unpredictable results, Offline Dice uses a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG). This means our algorithm is mathematically proven to be completely random, giving every color the exact same probability of landing on every single roll.
